05 — COMPONENTS

Core components

Three device archetypes drive every control. Each renders a 54px circular "bubble" on the map/graph and a card or table row in lists.

Toggle
On / Off devices — lights, fan, TV, sockets. Active = amber fill + glow + ON pill.
Slider
Graded devices — curtains, roller shutter. Reports "% Open" with amber-filled track.
Closed
Sensor
Read-only — door, window, presence, temperature. Colored dot + state pill, no controls.
Nav rail button
64px square, icon + label. Active = amber-tint fill, amber icon, cream label.
Category pill
Rounded. Selected gains a check glyph + warm fill (#F6E6C9 / #9A6A1E).
Segmented switcher
View + graph-mode toggles. Active segment = white chip with soft shadow.
Detail panel
380px white card, header + close. Two variants: device and rule.
06 — FEATURE SPEC

Graph view

Renders devices at their real floor-plan positions over the room photo, then overlays the automation network. Answers two questions a flat list can't: what controls what, and what's connected to the hub.

Dependencies
Sensors link by dashed lines into rule "gear" nodes; gears emit solid arrows to the device they control. Gear sits at the centroid of its connected devices.
Connections
Every device draws a live spoke to a central room hub. A simple topology read — what's paired and online.
INTERACTION & FOCUS
1Tap a gear → trace mode: only that rule's sensors + controlled devices stay lit, everything else dims to ~28%. Opens the rule-detail panel.
2Tap a device → opens the device-detail panel; its label pins on.
3Category filter → dims everything outside the chosen category, keeping the spatial map intact.
4Re-tap the active gear → clears trace mode and closes the panel.
07 — PATTERN

Rule grammar

Every automation reads as a three-part sentence. The detail panel renders it as stacked blocks so the logic is scannable without parsing prose.

Trigger
WHEN Presence Sensor reports Presence
Condition (optional)
AND Door Sensor is open
Action
Turn on the Door Light
Conditions are nullable — time-driven rules (e.g. "Curtain after 7pm") skip the middle block. Each rule also surfaces a plain-language summary and last-triggered timestamp.
08 — BEHAVIOR

States & motion

On / active glow
Amber fill + layered halo (0 0 22px rgba(224,153,47,.55)). Transitions at .2s.
Selected
3px amber ring on bubble; amber border + lift shadow on cards; warm row tint in tables.
Dimmed / out-of-focus
Opacity ~0.28, desaturated icon. Used by graph trace + category filter.
Live clock
Top-bar time refreshes every 10s; "last activity" stamps update to "just now" on control.
Radii bubbles 50% · cards 16px · panels 18px · pills 999px · chips 9px  ·  Transitions .12–.2s ease on color, shadow, transform.
